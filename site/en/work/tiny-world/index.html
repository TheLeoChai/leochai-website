A little about this world

A little scene to watch. The stills and replay use the same bundled fixture. Its actions, words, and routes are authored, not a captured live run or a claim about actual cognition or private memory. There is no backend, model inference, or audio.

Authored paths and compressed timing illustrate the sequence; these are not captured positions. The herbs are already grown. Seek to any moment; the completed scene and numbered story also work without JavaScript.
