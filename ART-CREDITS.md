# Little World art credits

Little World is an **authored illustration**. Its three adult residents, their
roles and their afternoon are fictional; the scene is not Leo's biography or a
recorded simulation. No image-generation model was used. No paid assets, copied
game characters, music, Miku artwork or logos are used.

| Source | Creator | License | Reuse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Tiny Farm 1.0](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-farm) | Kenney | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) | Garden beds, crops, tools, counters, crates and small objects |
| [Tiny Town 1.1](https://kenney.nl/assets/tiny-town) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | Workshop and kitchen tile pieces, trees, well, fences, paths and windows |
| [Puny Characters](https://merchant-shade.itch.io/16x16-puny-characters), [artist's OpenGameArt upload](https://opengameart.org/content/puny-characters) | Shade | CC0 1.0 | First two unarmed Character-Base frames, edited into three civilian residents |

The courtyard composition, original bridge/radio/sign details, terrain shapes,
restrained shared palette remap, civilian clothing/hair edits, and action/meal
overlays were authored for this website. The setting and presentation paths are
original composition assembled from those reusable licensed ingredients.

Unmodified source images and supplied Kenney license files live under
`vendor/little-world/`. Shade's archive supplies no standalone license file;
`vendor/little-world/puny/NOTICE.md` records the artist's explicit public CC0
declaration. `sources.json` pins exact public download URLs, archive SHA-256s,
member paths, image SHA-256s and the 2026-09-12 verification date. No license is
inferred from a third-party repost.

Both Kenney packed sheets are 192×176: 12×11 cells, 16×16 each, **zero gutter**.
Their alternate unpacked sheets have a one-pixel gutter; those are not used.
Shade's sheet is 768×256 with 32×32 cells, no gutter. Pixel cropping uses those
verified dimensions. The cropped front-facing body bounding box is
`[9,10,23,23]`; the sprite foot anchor is `[16,23]`.

Rebuild and review instructions: [art/little-world/README.md](art/little-world/README.md).
